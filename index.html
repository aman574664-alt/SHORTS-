LESS ADMIN. MORE MAKING.

Your next Short,
in a few steps.

01 / Connect

Open Connect YouTube. Set up your free Google Desktop app once, import its JSON, sign in, then enable reviewed uploads.

02 / Add finished videos

Choose one folder on your computer, external drive, or a locally synced Google Drive folder. Wait 30 seconds after the copy finishes. For slow syncs, use a .part ending until the file is complete, then rename it.

03 / Review & approve

Click a video. Edit its title, description, audience and time. Save details, then approve the upload. Choose Private to upload without publishing, or Schedule for a future public release.

04 / Leave the upload running

Resume the queue. Keep the computer awake and online until YouTube accepts the upload and schedule. A missed time is blocked, never changed to Publish now.

What counts as a Short?

Square or vertical videos up to three minutes qualify under YouTube’s current rules. YouTube makes the final classification. This app does not crop, transcode, or verify video dimensions.

Interrupted or uncertain?

Chunks resume in the same session. Expired or uncertain sessions are checked against your channel before any retry. “Needs review” blocks another copy. Never clear history just to retry.

Thumbnails

Choose a JPG or PNG under 2 MB before upload. The app tries YouTube’s thumbnail API. Shorts support depends on the channel and API; if rejected, use YouTube Studio.

Keep it local

Closing the app stops uploads and scanning. History stays saved. Pausing stops new chunks after the current request; already scheduled videos still publish on YouTube. Cancel an uploaded item to remove its schedule and keep it private.

This app uses YouTube API Services. You are responsible for your content and audience settings.
