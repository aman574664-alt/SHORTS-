How to use this preview
  1. Choose a folder containing finished MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM files.
  2. Wait at least 30 seconds after copying. Keep this window open.
  3. Review each title, description, audience and posting time. Press Save details.
  4. Press Start demo queue to simulate uploading.

Original videos are never moved or deleted. The app must be open for scanning. Once a future real upload is scheduled on YouTube, YouTube can publish while your PC is off.
