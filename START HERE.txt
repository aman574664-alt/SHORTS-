SHORTS STUDIO — START HERE

1. Double-click ShortsScheduler.exe in this folder.
2. Open Connect YouTube in the left sidebar.
3. Follow the Google setup links. Create a free Google Desktop OAuth app,
   download its JSON, import it, and sign in with your own Google account.
4. Enable reviewed uploads when ready.
5. Choose a folder of finished videos. Wait 30 seconds after copying.
6. Click a video, save its details and audience, then approve it.
7. Resume the queue. Keep the app open and your computer awake while uploading.

Choose Keep private for a private upload or Schedule public release for a future
public post. Scheduling asks for confirmation. Missed times are blocked.
YouTube can publish an accepted schedule while your PC is off.

Your original videos are never deleted or moved.
Keep all files in this application folder together.
No Python, paid hosting, or paid AI subscription is needed.

This build has been tested with mocked YouTube responses. Real sign-in/upload
requires your own channel and an explicitly approved first live test.
